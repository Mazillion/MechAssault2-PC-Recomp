Unmodified source references are retained in vendor/reference/.

Modifications: inference-only plain PyTorch modules replace MMCV registries,
ConvModule wrappers and training dependencies. Per-pair optical flow is batched
to reduce GPU memory use. Single-frame sequences are supported. The pipeline
adds bounded temporal context, scene boundaries, resumable atomic PNG batches,
configurable 2x/3x/4x final resolution, adjustable blending, duration presets,
and FFmpeg audio/video export.
EMA generator weights load strictly with all 320 checkpoint tensors matched.
The discriminator, optimizer and training losses are not instantiated.

Checkpoint is the author's linked RealBasicVSR.pth:
https://dl.dropboxusercontent.com/s/eufigxmmkv5woop/RealBasicVSR.pth
SHA256: f2d73c0fe3e33869c812f608be77cbdbfc356443b718b78c9a6cb97027ce5de7

FFmpeg is the build the installer downloads for the game (gyan.dev 9.0.2
full shared, GPL-3.0); its licence and README are installed in Licenses\.
PyTorch, NumPy and Pillow are reused from Enhancer/.venv.
