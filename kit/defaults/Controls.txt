MechAssault 2 on PC - controls, menus and settings
==================================================

Start the game with "MechAssault 2.exe" in the game folder (or the Desktop or
Start-menu shortcut).


MENUS AND MOUSE
---------------
- Enter, Space, Esc or a left click skips an intro movie. Skipping does not
  select anything in the next menu.
- Enter or a left click continues from the title screen.
- Enter selects a menu item, Esc goes back. On the main menu itself Esc asks
  whether to quit; on a message box Esc closes the message.
- Point at menu items and click to select them. The arrow keys and a
  controller work as well. The key hints at the bottom of a menu, for example
  [Esc] Back, light up under the pointer and can be clicked.
- In the main lobby, click the 3D scene above the menu to hide the pointer and
  turn the camera. Esc gives the pointer back without leaving the menu.
- During play Esc pauses. Click the game to capture the mouse again. F8 turns
  mouse capture on and off.
- Switching to another window pauses play and silences the game; it carries on
  when you come back. To keep it running, set PauseOnFocusLoss=0 and
  MuteInBackground=0 in your settings file (see FILES below).

Greyed out, because this PC version has nothing behind them: Conquest (main
menu), System Link and Xbox Live (Multiplayer), Multiplayer Options and Add
Guest (Player Options). Game Demos is replaced by Custom Scenarios.

Player Options: press 1 in the lobby (RB on a controller) or pause during
play. Video Settings, Volume and Controls hold the PC options, drawn in the
game's own style. On-screen prompts follow the device you used last and your
current key bindings.


CONTROLLER
----------
Xbox-style (XInput) controllers work as soon as they are plugged in, together
with keyboard and mouse. PlayStation and older DirectInput-only pads need a
tool such as Steam Input or DS4Windows.

Controls (with a controller in use) has "Invert right stick Y" and
"Vibration"; they are saved in your player profile. Each Controls page links
to the other device's page, so you can set up the controller with the mouse.
Vibration works on Xbox-style (XInput) controllers. It stops when you switch
to keyboard and mouse or leave the game window.

In the tutorials, "Start" is shown as Esc while you play with the keyboard,
because Esc is the PC pause key. Quick-time prompts show your key or mouse
button inside the game's button art.


KEYBOARD AND MOUSE
------------------
Default keys. Every row can be changed under Player Options > Controls >
Keyboard and mouse bindings (three pages; each row is named by what it does,
with the Xbox button in brackets).

  Key                What it does                           Xbox button
  -----------------  -------------------------------------  -----------------
  W A S D            move                                   left stick
  Mouse              aim and turn the camera                right stick
  Left mouse or V    fire                                   right trigger
  Right mouse or C   next weapon                            left trigger
  Space              jump jets; neurohack (quick-time)      A
  Q                  enter or leave a vehicle               Y
  F                  the game's B-button action             B
  R                  the game's X-button action             X
  1                  White button; Player Options in lobby  White (RB)
  2                  Black button                           Black (LB)
  Shift              left stick click (VTOL: climb)         left stick click
  Ctrl               right stick click (VTOL: descend)      right stick click
  Enter or Esc       pause / objectives                     Start
  Backspace          Back button (during play only)         Back
  Arrow keys         D-pad (fixed)                          D-pad
  I J K L            right stick (fixed)                    right stick

To change a key: choose the row, then press the new key or mouse button
("Press any key or mouse button..."). Esc cancels. Enter, Esc, F8 and F11 are
kept for the menus. If the key you choose is already used by another row, both
rows now use it: change the other row as well.

W A S D follow the key positions, so on an AZERTY or Dvorak keyboard they sit
where W A S D are on a US keyboard.

Mouse aiming is direct: no acceleration or dead zone, and it feels the same at
any frame rate. Controls also has mouse sensitivity, invert mouse, invert
flight controls, and keyboard and mouse on or off.


BOT MATCHES
-----------
Main menu > Multiplayer > Bot Match > New Game has three extra rows: Red Bots
and Blue Bots (or Bots in free-for-all modes) and Bot Skill. Bots play every
mode and map, including your own multiplayer maps, use every vehicle, and wait
while the game is paused.

A new profile starts with 3 Red bots, 4 Blue bots, 5 bots in free-for-all
modes and skill Mixed.

Team sizes: a team holds 6 players, you included, and you join the side the
counts leave short. For example 6 Red and 5 Blue puts you on Blue for 6
against 6; 1 Red and 4 Blue puts you on Red, 2 against 4. Free-for-all takes
up to 11 bot opponents (Off plays the map alone). Grinder is two-player
co-op: one bot partner at most.

Bot Skill: Green, Regular, Veteran, Elite, Dragoon (past Elite: reacts at once
and never lets up) or Mixed (a lobby spread: on each side one Dragoon ace,
Elites, Veterans, Regulars and Greens, with the team you face a notch
stronger). Skill changes reactions, aim and decisions only; every bot has the
same armour and weapons you would have.

More in Docs\Bots guide.txt.


CUSTOM SCENARIOS
----------------
"MA2 Scenario Editor.exe", next to the game, makes multiplayer maps and
single-player missions from your own disc's models and textures. It saves
.ma2map files to Documents\MechAssault 2\Scenarios (change the folder under
File > Settings > General in the editor).

- Multiplayer maps are listed in Multiplayer > Bot Match > New Game with the
  original maps, for every game type they support. A map saved while the game
  runs shows up the next time you open the Multiplayer menu.
- Missions are under Custom Scenarios on the main menu. Pick one with the
  arrows and press Enter. Custom missions keep their own save: your campaign
  progress is never touched.
- Two scenarios with the same name are listed with their file names.
- The editor's Test button (F5) saves and starts the game with that scenario.
  Test refuses to start while Check scenario shows red (Must fix) items.

More in Docs\Scenario editor.txt.


DISPLAY AND AUDIO
-----------------
Video Settings: window mode, resolution, textures, VSync, frame limit, aspect
ratio, smooth scaling, draw distance, frame-rate display, cutscene videos and
shader pre-loading. Click a row to step its value forward; right-click or Left
steps back. "Apply display settings" applies window changes; Back saves.

- The first launch picks borderless fullscreen at your desktop resolution.
- Resolution is the size the game renders at. In borderless fullscreen your
  desktop resolution does not change; the picture is scaled to the screen.
- F11 or Alt+Enter switches between window and fullscreen, also while the
  settings are open.
- Frame limit: 30 to 360 FPS, 144 by default.
- Textures: Original, 2x Enhanced or 4x Enhanced. Only packs made during the
  install are offered; without any it reads "(enhanced packs not installed)".
  The change takes effect in the current mission. The first use of a pack can
  pause briefly while textures load.
- Cutscene videos: plays the 2x enhanced movies if they were made during the
  install; otherwise Original.
- Pre-load shaders on loading screens: prepares the graphics a level is known
  to use while its loading screen is up, so new effects do not stutter the
  first time. It lengthens loading slightly; on by default.
- Draw distance: 0 to 100; 50 = the original, 0 = half, 100 = twice as far.
  Click or drag the bar, or use Left/Right in steps of five. Higher values can
  lower the frame rate. "Restore display defaults" sets it back to 50.
- Aspect ratio Automatic widens the 3D view for widescreen and ultrawide
  screens; Original 4:3 adds side bars. Movies keep their own shape.

Volume: master, effects, speech and music. Changes are heard at once and saved
when you leave the page.


COMMAND LINE
------------
  "MechAssault 2.exe" --scenario <file> --mode <m> --skill <n> --bots <r>v<b>
      starts a scenario file directly (what the editor's Test button does)
  "MechAssault 2.exe" --browse
      opens Custom Scenarios
  "MechAssault 2.exe" --skip-intro
      skips the intro movies


FILES
-----
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\MechAssault2\pc_settings.ini   your settings
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\MechAssault2\Saved Games       your saves
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\MechAssault2\Logs              logs, crash and hang reports,
                                                bot match recordings
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\MechAssault2\ShaderCache       graphics shader cache
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\MechAssault2\NavCache          the bots' route cache

The two cache folders and Logs can be deleted at any time; the game rebuilds
them. The pc_settings.ini beside the game only holds the first-launch
defaults. Closing the game window quits the game.
