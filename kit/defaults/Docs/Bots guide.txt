MechAssault 2 PC recompilation - bots guide (version {{VERSION}})
================================================================

The original game has no computer opponents in multiplayer. This PC version
adds offline bots that play every multiplayer mode on every map, including
your own maps made with the Scenario Editor.


WHERE THE SETTINGS ARE
----------------------
Main menu > Multiplayer > Bot Match > New Game. Besides the game type, map
and rules there are three rows:
- Red Bots and Blue Bots (team modes) or Bots (free-for-all modes): how many.
- Bot Skill: how good they are.
Each row explains itself while it is selected. A new profile starts with
3 Red, 4 Blue, 5 in free-for-all and skill Mixed.


TEAM SIZES
----------
A team holds 6 players, you included. The counts are exact, and you join the
side they leave short:
- 6 Red and 5 Blue: you join Blue, 6 against 6.
- 1 Red and 4 Blue: you join Red, 2 against 4.
Free-for-all: up to 11 bot opponents; Off plays the map alone.
Grinder is two-player co-op: one bot partner at most.


SKILL
-----
  Green      new pilots: slow to react, miss a lot
  Regular    steady
  Veteran    quick and sensible
  Elite      very good: fast reactions, accurate, uses the map well
  Dragoon    past Elite: reacts at once and never lets up
  Mixed      a lobby spread: on each side one Dragoon ace, Elites,
             Veterans, Regulars and Greens; the team you face is a notch
             stronger

Skill changes only reactions, aim and decisions. Every bot has the same
armour, health and weapons you would have in the same 'Mech; nobody gets
extra damage.


WHAT BOTS DO
------------
- Play the objective of each mode: carry and defend flags, attack and guard
  power plants, collect cores, take checkpoints, hunt the leader.
- Use every vehicle: walk 'Mechs, drive tanks, fly VTOLs (and carry
  BattleArmor riders), board vehicles on foot and leave them when they are
  about to blow.
- BattleArmor walk and use their jets for longer moves and to climb.
- Pick up power-ups on the way, retreat when overheated, escape the blast of
  a dying 'Mech, and come back after a respawn.
- Stand down while the game is paused.


WHAT THEY DO NOT DO WELL YET
----------------------------
See Docs\Known issues.txt (Bots). In short: VTOLs flying home under fire are
often lost; a tank that lands hard on a slope can stay stuck and fights from
there; on a few maps a bot can be caught for a moment near a base exit.


REPORTING A BOT PROBLEM
-----------------------
Run the installer again and press Collect diagnostics: it puts one zip on
your Desktop with the logs (your Windows user name removed). Attach that zip
to your report at {{MA2_SUPPORT_URL}} and note the time in the match when
the problem happened.

If a maintainer asks for a bot recording: set BotRecorder=1 under [Bots] in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\MechAssault2\pc_settings.ini, play until the problem
happens again, then run Collect diagnostics and tick "Include the newest
bot match recording". A recording (.botrec, in
the Logs folder) can be up to 150 MB, and the newest 4 are kept, so turn it
off again afterwards. Issue attachments are limited to about 25 MB: if the
zip is larger, the maintainer will tell you where to upload it.
