MechAssault 2 PC recompilation - known issues (version {{VERSION}})
==================================================================

Things we know about and have not fixed yet. If you meet something not on
this list, please report it at {{MA2_SUPPORT_URL}} (see Docs\Troubleshooting.txt
for what to send).


GAME
----
- Intel integrated graphics: a tinted picture at a low frame rate was seen on
  one laptop and is not yet explained. Reports from Intel and AMD graphics
  are very welcome.
- During a bot match there is no known keyboard key that shows the score
  table; it appears at the end of the match.
- A short pause can occur when the game saves a checkpoint.
- Custom missions: walk and patrol orders can be pulled off route by an enemy
  within about 450 m.
- Custom missions: Defend picked from the Order drop-down only guards the
  spot; use the orders chooser to walk there first.
- Mission 2: one unexplained close to the desktop was seen once and has not
  been reproduced. If it happens to you, send the log.
- Only the US (NTSC) retail disc is supported, and only English menus have
  the new PC texts (for example "BOT MATCH").
- Choosing a key that another binding already uses leaves both actions on
  that key; change the other row too.
- Not tested yet on: AMD graphics, Windows 10 without Visual Studio, non-English
  Windows, two monitors, HDR.


BOTS
----
- A tank that lands hard on a slope can stay stuck; its crew fights from
  there.
- VTOLs flying home under fire are often shot down.
- On a few maps bots can get caught for a moment in a corner near a base
  exit (Snatch It: Killing Fields, No Man's Land east entrance, Team Last Man
  Standing: New River City blue start).
- A VTOL carrying a BattleArmor rider sometimes circles its base for up to a
  minute before setting off.
- Bots follow the game's own movement code, which makes short stop-and-go
  jerks.
- A rare audio crash has been seen on the map Going Commando.


SCENARIO EDITOR
---------------
- Scenarios saved with an editor older than 1.0.2 can show pink markers in
  the game until they are saved once with the current editor (the editor
  offers to update them).
- Very large maps have had little testing.
- Maps with a cut road that were saved with editor 1.0.9 can crash the game
  when loading. Open them in this editor and save them again (Check scenario
  warns about them until you do).
- Retail sound cues cannot be played in the editor (Play is greyed out); your
  own sound clips can.
- A cutscene that can play twice uses hard cuts instead of fades.
- A briefing of more than six lines (several pages) has not been played in the
  game.
- Snow flakes close to the camera look larger and softer in the editor than in
  the game.


INSTALLER
---------
- The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen warns (see
  Docs\Install guide.txt).
- The one-click Microsoft Build Tools install has not yet been tried on a PC
  that never had Visual Studio.
- Enhanced textures on NVIDIA RTX 50-series cards have not been tested.
- HD textures on AMD and Intel graphics cards are new and haven't been
  tested on those cards.
- Enhanced cutscene videos on AMD and Intel graphics cards are new (1.1.3)
  and haven't been tested on those cards. They are made frame by frame,
  so they take much longer, and may flicker more than the NVIDIA version.
- The cutscene player uses a full GPL build of FFmpeg, which the installer
  downloads during the install (about 245 MB unpacked; the game uses five of
  its DLLs). A small decoder-only build is planned.
