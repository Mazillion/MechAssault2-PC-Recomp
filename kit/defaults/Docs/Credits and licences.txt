MechAssault 2 PC recompilation - credits and licences (version {{VERSION}})
==========================================================================

THE PROJECT
-----------
An unofficial fan project that turns your own MechAssault 2 disc into a PC
game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, Xbox, Day 1 Studios
or the BattleTech/MechWarrior rights holders. MechAssault and Xbox are
trademarks of their respective owners.

No game data files are included: the game's program, art, sound and levels
come from your own disc. Graphics shaders are compiled on your PC as the game
meets them, so a level's first visit can hitch briefly; nothing compiled from
the game is included.

The project's own code (the PC layer, the offline bots, scenario support, the
installer scripts and the MA2 Scenario Editor) is released under the MIT
licence: Licenses\LICENSE.txt.


BUILT WITH
----------
- xboxrecomp, the toolkit that translates the Xbox program to C and provides
  the runtime (MIT): https://github.com/sp00nznet/xboxrecomp
- Parts of xemu's audio and graphics emulation (LGPL 2.1 or later; espes,
  Jannik Vogel, Matt Borgerson): https://github.com/xemu-project/xemu
- A DSP56300 emulator for the audio DSP (GPL).
- libsamplerate, xxHash, jsmn, DirectXTex's DDS loader, Dear ImGui.
- FFmpeg 9.0.2 for cutscene playback (GPL version 3, full build by
  gyan.dev; downloaded from gyan.dev during the install, not part of the
  installer). Source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/commit/946fcce07b ;
  build scripts and library sources: https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
- The installer runs on Python 3.13 and uses Capstone; its window uses the
  Rajdhani and Share Tech Mono fonts (SIL Open Font Licence).
- Optional enhanced content uses Real-ESRGAN, BasicSR, RealBasicVSR, PyTorch
  and texconv, downloaded during the install; without an NVIDIA card the
  textures use Real-ESRGAN's realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan (MIT, built on ncnn).

Every component with its version, licence and licence file is listed in
Licenses\THIRD-PARTY.txt; the licence texts are in the Licenses folder.
